# Hopwell: references for the 2026-11-15 simplify

Refero has no small chiropractors. These four health and booking sites each lead with one idea, and I took one structural trait from each. None of their look came with it. Stone and Sage, Albert Sans and the neumorphism Tristan asked for all stay Hopwell's own.

| Reference | What it does | The one trait borrowed |
|---|---|---|
| [Hims UK home](https://refero.design/pages/b1aca96c-da1a-46d0-aedc-8611570697ae) | One large headline and one way in, with no form on the first screen | The first screen is "Point to where it hurts." and the photo, and nothing else. The paragraph of instructions, the chips and the result card are gone from the top. |
| [Hers question screen](https://refero.design/pages/d1f3c27d-43ea-43c7-8439-7c20522a5722) | One question alone, with a few stacked answers | Question 2 appears only after you tap the photo, question 3 only after question 2, and the answer card only after the third answer |
| [Hers medical intake](https://refero.design/pages/407159e3-5505-4036-b10e-f2cf53afdaeb) | A question, a line of help and the answers, with nothing chosen yet | The page arrives empty. Nothing is pre-chosen and nothing goes into booking until you have answered all three. |
| [Fresha venue page](https://refero.design/pages/3f9866ee-1a97-4940-8266-de49f1158248) | One booking card beside the list of services and prices | Booking sits beside "What a visit costs", and the hook's answers feed straight into it |

## What was cut

These come from the critic's notes in sections A1, A8, B2, B3, C3 and G:

- The pre-filled answers ("low back, 2–6 weeks, sitting") and the copy claiming forms were "already started"
- The "Live insurance check" and the member-ID checker, replaced by the plain list of plans
- The first-visit steps with three photos, which repeated "Visits and prices"
- The second copy of the three questions in the intake form. Intake is now two steps: how much it hurts right now, and who the form is for.
- "Who would you like to see?" in booking, which defaulted to the first available doctor anyway. Booking is now three steps.
- A review that described the website, rewritten to describe the visit

## What stayed

- The neumorphism, using the same two tokens, so each shadow follows the radius of the chip, button or card it sits on
- The warning-signs check inside the answer card
