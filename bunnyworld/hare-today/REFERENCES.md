# Hare Today: references for the one-idea pass (2026-11-16)

Why: an outside visitor's favourites (Bunny Burritos' "BIG BURRITOS", Bunny Auto's "SERVICE & REPAIR") open on one claim in huge type. The critic (notes/bunny-world/critic/REVIEW.md, A6, B2, B3, B6, C1, E and G) found Hare Today's headline sharing the stage with a chair board that is a grey slab of CLOSED most hours, no faces, an announcement bar contradicting the status line, and Sundays advertised but not bookable.

Borrowed: structure and restraint only. Nothing of their look, colour, type or branding is used.

| Reference (Refero) | What its first screen does | The one trait borrowed |
|---|---|---|
| GlossGenius barber software ([Refero](https://refero.design/pages/38c32116-7b02-449b-8291-8293cc929d31)) | One big plain headline, one sentence, one main action beside one photo | A plain trade headline ("Cuts & shaves in Berthoud.") with one button, "Book a chair" |
| Fresha venue page, AMA Brixton ([Refero](https://refero.design/pages/60fd6a48-8164-43cb-af35-137f40b7ee74)) | The name, then one quiet line: "Open until 8:00 PM · address · Get directions" | Open status as one line of words under the headline, instead of a live board in the hero |

| The same Fresha page, its Team row | A row of face photos with name and role, each person bookable | The barbers as faces, each card with its own "Book with ___" |

## What was cut or folded

- The red announcement bar ("Sundays are open now") that sat over "Closed · opens Tuesday". Sundays now live in one sentence under the headline, and Sundays can be booked.
- The phone quick bar (Open today / Call / Map / Book) and the header "Book now" button. Booking is offered once at the top.
- The chair board in the hero, its pulsing LIVE light and the "Show a busy Saturday" second hero button. Who's free now lives in the barbers section, and the busy-Saturday demo is a quiet text link there.
- The pop-out booking drawer, a second booking path that did the same job as the booking block.
- The four tiny "Book Gus" links (55 × 18 px), now full-size "Book with ___" buttons on each barber's card.
- The menu filter chips (nine items don't need filtering).
- Clipped lines ("Sundays are optional. So is the playlist. Mostly.", "Twelve and under.") rewritten as full sentences.
- The eyebrow "Book with your barber" over the title "Book with anyone".
