Switching to us

The questions everyone asks before they switch

We build the new site on a private preview link first, and you check it on your phone. Nothing goes live until you say yes. The switch itself is a settings change at your domain; we pick a quiet hour for it and keep the old site ready, in case we need to switch back.

Yes, your domain stays in your name. We only point the website part of it at the new site, so your email keeps working. Your Google Business Profile stays yours too, and we can update its website link and hours for you.

We carry over the pages, words and photos you want to keep, and tell you what we would leave behind and why. Nothing gets thrown away without you seeing it first.

Your domain, your words and your photos are yours. What you own of the site itself, and what happens if you leave, is written into your quote before you pay anything.

The care plan, from $50 a month, covers hosting, security and the small changes like new hours, a new menu or a sale. You text Tristan or Bryce and we make the change. If you want something bigger, we’ll give you a fixed price for it first.

  1. TryPick the shop on Main Street that’s closest to yours and look around on your phone. Make a note of what you’d keep and what you’d change. That list is the best way to start our call.
  2. TalkThen we’ll talk for twenty minutes, with Tristan or Bryce, not a form or a chatbot. By the end you’ll know the price and the date, and we’ll send both to you in writing.
  3. BuildWe build it and send you drafts to open on your phone. Tell us what’s off and we’ll fix it until it’s right. Nothing goes live until you’re happy with it.
