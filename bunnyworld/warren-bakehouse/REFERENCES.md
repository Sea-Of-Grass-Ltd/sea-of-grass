# Warren Bakehouse: references for the 2026-11-15 simplify

Refero has almost no small bakeries, so these are restrained food and café sites whose first screen leads with one idea. I took one structural trait from each and none of their look. The palette, Young Serif, cream and roasted brown all stay Warren's own.

| Reference | What its first screen does | The one trait borrowed |
|---|---|---|
| [ARKET Café](https://refero.design/pages/04f6420a-77ea-4911-a6de-3314f4b8f79c) | One photo and one short line, with nothing to operate | The first screen asks you to feel something, not do something. The clock, the 60× simulator and the live counters are gone. |
| [ARKET Stores & cafés](https://refero.design/pages/064d7aa4-4434-44d8-9ea9-9ac1a45f8f78) | A big serif heading on the left and one photo on the right, with the details further down | A huge headline beside one photo, and the hours moved down to "Come by" |
| [Douze home](https://refero.design/pages/595312b9-fe62-4b75-81b4-a1cfdda61162) | A split hero, then one plain grid of products | One list for everything in the case. "On the rack" and "The rest of the case" are merged and keep the filter chips. |
| [The Dinner Ladies home](https://refero.design/pages/08744583-3634-489c-b170-814d03aaad02) | Plain menu cards (a name, a line about the food, a price) in a warm, first-person voice | Each item has a name, the kind of bread, one straight line about the food and a price. There are three jokes, not sixteen, and the baker has a name (Hazel). |

## What was cut

These come from the critic's notes in sections B3, C2, E and G:

- The countdown clock, the "Watch a morning at 60×" button and the fake live counts
- The dot rows, the wrapper labels, the sold-out stamps, the per-card colour stripes, and the sticker on the photo
- The pale blue announcement bar
- The second product grid and the case photo
- The Coffee section, which is now one sentence under "Come by"

The cake builder stays, because the lettering on the cake is the page's one fun moment. It now asks for a phone number, which the critic flagged in B5.
