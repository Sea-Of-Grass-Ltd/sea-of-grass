    Questions people ask

    How far ahead should I order?

    A box of pastries needs one day, a cake needs two, and a wedding cake needs about a month.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes, within five miles for orders over $60.

    Is anything nut-free?

    We bake with nuts in the same kitchen, so we can't promise that anything is nut-free. The gluten-free banana loaf is baked first, before any wheat flour comes out.

    Run a bakery or café? Your site can do all of this.
