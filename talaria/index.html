Talaria

The studio’s task manager, named for the winged sandals of Hermes. Projects as lists or boards, shared live between Sea of Grass and Caduceus, and reachable from Claude.

First launch

macOS. Open the .dmg and drag Talaria into Applications. The app isn’t notarized by Apple, so the first time, right-click it and choose Open, then Open again. After that it opens normally.

Linux. Make the AppImage executable with chmod +x Talaria_*.AppImage and run it, or install the .deb or .rpm with your package manager.

Claude. Download the Claude extension and open it; the Claude app shows an Install button. Then ask Claude things like “what’s on my plate this week?” or “add a task to Touch Grass for Bryce, due Friday”. The first time, your browser opens to sign in with Google, and Claude only sees what you can see. It can add and change tasks but never delete them.

Signing in. Click Continue with Google and pick the Gmail you were invited with. You’ll only see the spaces that address belongs to.
