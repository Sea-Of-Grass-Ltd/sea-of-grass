Talaria privacy

Talaria is a private task manager used by the people at Sea of Grass and the Caduceus study group. It isn’t a public service, and nothing in it is sold, shared or used for advertising.

What it stores

  • Your account: your name, email address and profile photo. When you sign in with Google, Talaria receives only these, never your password or anything else in your Google account.
  • Your work: the projects, tasks, comments and photos you and your teammates add.
  • On your computer: a sign-in session, so you stay signed in. The Claude extension keeps its own session in a file only your user account can read.

Who can see it

Work is divided into spaces. Only people whose email has been added to a space can see anything in it. Profile photos are stored at public, unguessable addresses so they can be shown in the app.

Where it lives

Everything is stored with Supabase, which hosts Talaria’s database. Sign-in with Google is handled by Google under its own privacy policy. If you connect Talaria to Claude, Claude reads and changes your tasks only when you ask it to, under Anthropic’s privacy policy.

Removing your data

Ask Tristan at Sea of Grass and your account and everything only you created will be deleted.
