## The hook: "The August Test"
Northern Colorado's real yard problem is water: watering restrictions, brown lawns by late summer, and city turf-replacement rebates. So the page leads with a live test of your own yard against the calendar.

1. **Your yard:** pick a size (a slider in square feet, or Small / Medium / Big lot) and what's there now (thirsty turf, a patchy lawn, or bare dirt).
2. **Drag the months:** a Jan → Dec scrubber. The photo pair swaps between "Turf" and "Water-wise" for the same yard, and the numbers update live: gallons this month, water bill, mowing hours, and a "how it looks" note. In August the turf side goes brown and the bill peaks, while the water-wise side stays gold and green. That's the moment.
3. **The year in one line:** "Switching saves about 38,000 gallons and $410 a year, and 31 hours of mowing." Demo maths from typical Front Range figures, clearly labelled as an estimate.
4. **Rebate check:** your zip → the city turf-replacement rebate that might apply ("Loveland: about $1 a square foot, up to $3,000", demo figures, with a note to confirm with your city). It sets the net cost against what you save, then hands straight to the quote form with the yard size and choice already filled in.

Why it works: it's specific to the place, it's interactive in seconds, and it turns the firm's best line into proof. It also makes the quote form warmer, because the visitor arrives with numbers.

**Second hook, seasonal:** a sprinkler **blowout and start-up route booker**. Pick your neighbourhood and see the days the crew is already nearby. A "crew's on your street" day earns $15 off. Slots fill up, with a sold-out state, as in the other shops. It's very real for Colorado in October and April.
