# Long Ears: one direction from Refero ("Fieldwork") — for Tristan's pick, nothing built

Tristan: "IDK check Refero for Landscaping ideas". Refero has no landscaping companies, so these are the closest outdoor, garden and earth-toned brands. What makes each one work:

1. **Raus** (raus.life, nature cabins): a warm cream canvas, a chunky rounded forest-green wordmark, rounded photo cards and a sky-blue offer bar. It feels premium but friendly and outdoorsy, not corporate.
2. **arte\*** ("fieldwork meets pop", makearte.ca, farm to table): a full-bleed photo of real work happening (a greenhouse), an oversized lowercase wordmark in one sunny accent, and typewriter-style labels. Confidence comes from the work itself.
3. **Shelby Kay** ("earthbound typographic choreography"): cream, olive, moss and clay; outsized grotesk with tiny matter-of-fact text. The palette lesson.
4. **Until Labs** ("science with soft edges"): a lush green full-bleed photo with a tight grotesk headline and cream chapters. Proof that green photography plus one plain headline is enough.
5. **Aker** ("landscapes and ledgers"): thin white route lines, light caps, an institutional ledger. **This is what Long Ears copies today**, which is why it reads generic and borrowed.
6. **Postevand** ("ruled object theatre"): grainy outdoor photography, huge regular grotesk, fine rules. A lesson in restraint around a big photo.

## The direction: "Fieldwork"
Lead from **arte\*** (real work, full-bleed, one sunny accent, typewriter labels) and **Raus** (cream canvas, chunky friendly wordmark, rounded cards). Drop Aker's lines and ledger.

- **Type:** **Bricolage Grotesque** 800, lowercase, for the wordmark and headings: chunky, characterful and a little hand-made, unused by any other shop. **Hanken Grotesk** for text (already on the page), **IBM Plex Mono** for labels and numbers. No serif, no Lora.
- **Palette:** cream `#F5F0E3` base, deep pine `#1E3326` ink, **prairie gold `#F0C94A`** as the one sunny accent (the wordmark, numbers, the quote button), Colorado sky `#A9CBE6` for small info chips (prices, the blowout bar), clay `#C27A4E` only for the turf side of the August Test.
- **Hero treatment:** a full-bleed photo of the water-wise yard in August (the current drawing stands in until the photos; its mountains go). The giant gold lowercase "long ears" sits on the photo, with a mono pill label above it, and an **August Test teaser card** sits on the photo itself (turf vs water-wise gallons, "Try it on your yard"), so the hook shows above the fold.
- **Sections:** rounded white cards on cream, a gold number dot, a Bricolage title, Hanken text, and a sky-blue "from $X" chip on every service (the starting prices are placeholders).
