# Cottontail Farm (seasonal): photo shot list (waiting on Tristan's "go")

The hero changes with the season, so the photo does too. The look: warm, golden, late-afternoon farm light, shot on 35 mm film, honest and uncrowded, never stocky. The recurring bunny prop is a small weathered wooden rabbit (a fence-post carving), in every shot, off to the side.

Never in the frame: faces (people only from far away or from behind), text, signage, logos, real brands. Nothing of the prospect farm's is copied: not the name, logo, photos or copy.

1. **Summer hero (16:9):** a field of tall sunflowers at golden hour, faces turned to the low sun, a mown path leading in, the wooden rabbit on a fence post in the foreground. Warm yellow and deep green.
2. **Fall hero (16:9):** a pumpkin patch at golden hour, pumpkins on the vine, hay bales, a tall corn maze wall behind, the wooden rabbit on a bale. Orange and dusty gold.
3. **Off-season hero (16:9):** the resting field at first light in November: frost on stubble, a red barn far off, long shadows, the wooden rabbit on a fence post with frost on its ears. Quiet olive and cream.
4. **Corn maze from above (4:3, the farm map section):** a drone view straight down over a corn maze with winding paths (an abstract pattern, no letters or logos in the maze), in late-afternoon light.
5. **The hayride (3:2, field trips):** an old flatbed wagon stacked with hay bales on a dirt lane at the field edge, no people, warm light, the wooden rabbit riding on a bale.
