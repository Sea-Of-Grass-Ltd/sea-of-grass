# Abuelita's Empanadas: style guide (v8, 2026-11-18)

**Their shop on the best day of its life.** It should look like a neighbourhood poster, not an old web page.

## References (Refero)
- **Lead: Roberta's Pizza, `3bccc923-6f39-4c02-a52b-aa994ea82253`.** A neighbourhood poster. Plain grounds, with red kept for actions and one loud chapter. Poster-scale type, sturdy label-shaped buttons with small corners (not pills), flat colour with no gradients, and a dark closing stage.
- **Borrowed: OLIPOP, `1bfb0316-3a14-4742-a75f-503c6a25d32d`.** Products sit unboxed on the cream like objects on a shelf, not inside white cards. Colour fills whole scenes instead of being scattered as accents. Category pills are outlined.
- **Rejected from both:** their fonts, halftone dot fields (a filler texture), the pink, the sticker art and the photo hero. We keep Abuelita's own pieces.

## Type
- **Lobster Two italic:** only "Abuelita's" and "Abuelita knows best".
- **Alfa Slab One capitals:** "EMPANADAS", every section head and every button label (like the poster labels).
- **Figtree:** everything else; item names in 700.

## Shapes
- **Buttons:** 8px corners, slab capitals at 15–17px, 54px tall. Red fill for the main action, maroon outline for the second.
- **Panels** (order step, coffee list): 2px maroon rule, 10px corners, flat.
- **Menu items:** unboxed. The photo sits on the cream with its round red + on the corner and the name under it. No card box and no shadow, except the photo's own drop shadow.
- **Stages:** full-bleed bands, not floating inset cards. The woven band marks the edges between chapters.

## Page rhythm
1. **Hero:** cream poster lettering over the black-board arch. Horchata, jamaica and chile relleno sit on it with white steam.
2. **Woven band.**
3. **Favourites:** a full red band with "Abuelita knows best" and the gold-framed trio.
4. **Savoury, appetisers, combo and sweet:** on cream, unboxed.
5. **Desserts:** a full maroon band with the woven top edge; the combo is a full black band.
6. **Coffee and drinks:** the cup art beside a ruled list.
7. **Order:** one question at a time.
8. **Visit:** a full black closing stage, with the woven band on top.

## Don'ts
- No CSS gradients as surfaces; gradients are made with mesh in Illustrator.
- No teal fills.
- No pills on main buttons.
- No soft drop-shadow cards.
- No scattered dots or stars.
