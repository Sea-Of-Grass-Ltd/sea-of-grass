01 · About

Who we are

Sea of Grass is Tristan Brewer and Bryce Nielsen. The name is what the plains were called before the towns.

We meet you at your shop, not on a sales call. We take the photos, write it with you, and build a site that works on a phone.

  • Twelve years designing for real businesses: brand, signage, email, web and social for a five-brand retail chain (149 to 329 stores).
  • This fall we looked at about 200 business sites across Loveland, Windsor, Wellington, Greeley and Berthoud.
