Nine directions

From the buyer research, November 23. These are directions to argue with, and you two draw the marks. Argue first with what you know about owners here.

  1. It has to read from across the road. That has the best evidence behind it.
  2. It should say what the shop does, with the mark or a plain line.
  3. Every shop gets its own colors, and no two sit close together.
  4. One bold idea each. Skip dark brown with cream and amber, skip mountains, and use only plants that grow here.
  5. Hand-drawn, retro, color meanings and mascots have weak evidence, so use them because you like them.

Long EarsLandscaping

First
Stands for
Owner with a few trucks adding water-wise work

A changing service still looks like one crew

Very broad letters seen at speed. Deep spruce, chalk and a clay-rose signal. Territory: ears as growth or direction. Only native plants, if any.
