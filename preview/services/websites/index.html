Questions

Before you buy

Anything else? Email us or call 303.775.1333.

What counts as one fix?

One thing on your site that is broken, wrong or out of date: wrong hours, a form that doesn’t send, a page that falls apart on phones. If yours turns out bigger than one fix, we say so and agree the price before any work.

What happens after I pay?

We call you within one business day to hear what you need. Then you get what we’ll do and when, in writing, before any work starts.

Can I cancel the care plan?

Yes, any month.

What if a Kickoff isn’t a fit?

If it’s not a fit after the call, you get a full refund. If you go ahead, the Kickoff is credited toward your brand or full-site project.

Do I have to move my site to you?

No. We can fix it where it is; hosting with us is only part of the care plan.

Who owns the site?

You do: the files, the domain and the logins.

Rather talk first?

Fifteen or thirty minutes. Tell us what you have; you leave with a next step and a price.
