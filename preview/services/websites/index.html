Hooking up

Hooking up, plainly

Connecting your register is the part that makes owners nervous, so here is exactly what happens, start to finish.

01

What it involves

One of us sits down with you, and you tap Connect on Square’s own page and approve it. It takes about two minutes, and you never give us your password.

02

What changes for you

Nothing about the way you work the counter changes. Online orders show up on your register already paid, so nobody has to take them over the phone, you never log in to anything to keep the site current, and your menu and prices stay in Square, where you already keep them.

03

How to undo it

Open your Square Dashboard, find our app and press disconnect, and that’s it. Your site keeps working with a call-to-order button, and the site, domain and files stay yours.

What we can see: your orders and the items on your menu, because that’s what the website needs to show them.

What we never see: your Square password, your bank details or any customer’s card, because Square handles every payment.

If you change your mind at the hookup, you owe nothing. We’d rather you feel sure than feel rushed.

Questions

Before you buy

Is there anything else you want to know? You can email us or call us on 303.775.1333.

I don’t have time for this.

That’s the point. We do the work, and the hookup takes about two minutes with one of us sitting beside you.

We already have a Facebook page.

Keep it. A real website is what shows up when people search for you, and we can link the two together.

I don’t need more business.

Then we keep it simple: your menu and hours kept right, or online ordering you can switch off whenever you’re busy.

What’s the catch?

There isn’t one. It’s $50 a month from day one, and you can cancel any time.

Which registers do you work with?

Square is ready today, and we’ve tested it from the menu all the way to the checkout. Shopify and Clover work too, and we set them up for each shop after checking your plan, because a few plans don’t allow it.

Does connecting Square cost anything?

We don’t charge for the hookup itself. Square takes its usual card fee on online orders, much like the one you already pay at the counter, and your Square Dashboard shows the exact rate.

What if I switch registers later?

Tell us and we’ll move the site over to the new one. The site, the domain and the files are yours either way.

What counts as one fix?

One thing on your site that is broken, wrong or out of date: wrong hours, a form that doesn’t send, a page that falls apart on phones. If yours turns out bigger than one fix, we say so and agree the price before any work.

What happens after I pay?

We call you within one business day to hear what you need. Then you get what we’ll do and when, in writing, before any work starts.

Can I cancel the care plan?

Yes, you can cancel it in any month.

How much does the care plan cover?

It covers hosting, security and up to 30 minutes of small changes each month, like new hours or a new menu. If a job will take longer, we’ll give you a fixed price for it before we start.

What if a Kickoff isn’t a fit?

If it’s not a fit after the call, you get a full refund. If you go ahead, the Kickoff is credited toward your brand or full-site project.

Do I have to move my site to you?

No, we can fix it where it is. Hosting with us is only part of the care plan.

Who owns the site?

You own the files, the domain and the logins.

Do I get anything beyond the site?

Every shop we build joins the Sea of Grass local network, so you’re listed in our upcoming guide to Front Range businesses at no extra cost.
