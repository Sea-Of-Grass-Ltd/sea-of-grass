Sea of Grass

Studies for Fantasmagoria

Browser studies for the controls of a live-theatre animation tool in progress, ahead of its Godot 4 build: one 3D street, three ways to perform it, plus a field you play with your hands.

  • Fever cast spells on a street
  • Make-Up redraw it in hand-made looks
  • Stutter play it like an instrument
  • Touch Grass move a field with your hands
Designed & built by Tristan Brewer tristan.design · seaofgrass.studio
