Questions

What if I miss a pickup?

Text us by noon and we will hold it a day. After that it goes to the food bank.

Can I pay in two parts?

Yes: half now, half on June 1.

Are dogs allowed in the patch?

On a leash, yes. Rabbits are already here.

Is it all organic?

We farm without synthetic sprays. We are not certified, because the paperwork costs more than the farm.
