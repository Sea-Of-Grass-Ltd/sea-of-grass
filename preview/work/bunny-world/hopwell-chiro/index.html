Your first visit

An hour, and no surprises.

  1. We talk

    What hurts, when it started, what you’ve tried. If you used “Where it hurts”, we already know the basics.

  2. We check how you move

    Posture, range of motion, a few gentle tests. We only adjust if it will help, and we say what we’re doing first.

  3. You leave with a plan

    How many visits we think it takes, what it costs with your plan, and two stretches to do at home.
