Grass shapes

Five shapes, all live. Move your pointer or finger through each row to throw the grass.

1. TrianglesWhat the site uses now.
2. Bent bladesA thin blade that curves at the tip and bends with the wind.
3. HairlinesOne fine line per blade, like a pen drawing.
4. TuftsThree short strokes from one root, like a clump.
5. Catnip leavesSmall pointed leaves, echoing the Duchess border.
