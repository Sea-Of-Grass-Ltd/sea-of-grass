Make-Up

The same endless street as Fever, for colour. Fever changes the shape of things; Make-Up changes their colour and leaves their shape alone. Press Make-Up and the whole scene puts on a look: Spectrum (oil-slick colour), Bleach (the colour drained out), Print (loose riso inks, out of register), Gloss (inflated chrome) or Strokes (bright crawling strands). Then click any thing to give it a look of its own, or leave it bare.

make-up
the button, or M: the scene puts on its look; press again and it comes off
look
the button beside Make-Up chooses the scene's look
click
click a thing (a colour of house, the lamps, the road, the lawn, the sky) and choose what it wears: the scene's look, a look of its own, or bare. Undress all, in controls, puts everything back
controls
the looks' dials, the speed you walk at, and Freeze

Make-Up is the colour tool from Fantasmagoria, the studio's live show, made to be used alongside Fever, and tried out here on its own. The street comes from a popular dreamcore meme; it is drawn live with three.js. Spectrum is after the colour of Fabio Catapano's Spectrum 001; Print after a clip of a riso-printed houseplant; Gloss after an airbrushed chrome flower; Strokes after a clip of bright strands crawling over a black stone. Nothing leaves your browser.
