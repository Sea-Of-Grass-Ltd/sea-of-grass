Make-Up

The same endless street as Fever, for colour. Fever changes the shape of things; Make-Up changes their colour and leaves their shape alone. Press Make-Up and put a look on the street. Spectrum floods what the pointer passes with oil-slick colour that drifts and shifts as you look at it from different angles; Bleach drains the colour out, and it seeps back slowly behind you; Print reprints it in a few loose spot inks, out of register, with black that breaks into dashes; Gloss turns it to glossy, inflated chrome; Strokes paints bright strands that crawl along the surfaces. Choose with the look button beside Make-Up.

make-up
the button, or M: puts the look on; press again and the street comes back as it was
steer
the look sits on whatever is under the pointer, with a wake behind it; click and it flares
pick
in controls, press Pick (or K), then click one thing: a colour of house, the lamps, the road, the lawn, the sky. The look then goes only on that. Clear lets it go on everything
look
the button beside Make-Up opens the list of looks; on the sky, each has a flat form
controls
turn the look's dials, the speed you walk at, and under Cast: flare, freeze, follow

Make-Up is the colour tool from Fantasmagoria, the studio's live show, made to be used alongside Fever, and tried out here on its own. The street comes from a popular dreamcore meme; it is drawn live with three.js. Spectrum is after the colour of Fabio Catapano's Spectrum 001; Print after a clip of a riso-printed houseplant; Gloss after an airbrushed chrome flower; Strokes after a clip of bright strands crawling over a black stone. Nothing leaves your browser.
