    Make-Up

    The same endless street as Fever, made up. Fever changes the shape of things; Make-Up changes how they look, never their shape. It works like the layers in an image editor: stack effects over the street, and each one works on everything beneath it.

    make-up
    the button, or M: shows the layers over the street, or takes them off
    + effect
    adds a layer on top: spray, airbrush, watercolour, glow, vector, scribble, pencil, chalk, ink, crayon, neon, print, gloss and more
    a layer
    the eye shows or hides it; the mode blends it with what's below (multiply, screen, overlay…); opacity sets how much; ↑ ↓ move it; × deletes it. Select it to change its settings
    mask
    with a layer selected, click things in the street (a colour of house, the lamps, the road, the lawn, the sky) and the layer only touches those. Click again to take one out; everything clears the mask

    Make-Up is the look tool from Fantasmagoria, the studio's live show, made to be used alongside Fever, and tried out here on its own. The street comes from a popular dreamcore meme, drawn live with three.js. The effects are after references Tristan collected: airbrushed posters, a stencil-sprayed house, a glowing neon swirl, a glossy vector flower, a riso-printed plant, Fabio Catapano's Spectrum 001, a board of scribbled, rotoscoped and crayoned animation, watercolour blots, a scribbled-pencil fill and a chalky brushed elephant. Nothing leaves your browser.
