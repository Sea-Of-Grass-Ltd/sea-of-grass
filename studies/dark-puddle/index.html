Dark Puddle

Your recently viewed websites, laid out as a map and fed into a cyclone that eats them. Newest sites are crisp at the top; older ones tear apart and spiral into the eye. Move the pointer to steer it.

Your history stays in this tab. Websites can't see your history, so you hand it over: export it from your browser and drop the file here. It's read inside this page, never uploaded or saved, and it's gone when you close the tab. If you're on a shared or projected screen, remember everyone can see it.

Pictures of the sites are optional. To show what each website looks like, the page asks Microlink, a screenshot service, for a picture of each site's home page (up to 24). That sends Microlink the site names, but not the pages you visited or when. Leave it off and each site is drawn as a coloured plot instead.

  1. Safari: File → Export Browsing Data, tick History, unzip the result, drop History.json.
  2. Chrome: takeout.google.com, choose Chrome, download, drop BrowserHistory.json.

The world-eating cyclone is after a TouchDesigner piece posted to r/TouchDesigner ("using TD to eat up worlds"): a scrolling map, a feedback loop, tearing rows and a whirlpool. Rebuilt here in WebGL.
