The cyclone is after a TouchDesigner piece posted to r/TouchDesigner ("using TD to eat up worlds"), rebuilt in WebGL. The controller borrows the control surface of tristan.design. Site pictures come from WordPress's mshots, which receives the addresses being shown; for a history file, only the site names. Your links and settings stay in this browser.
