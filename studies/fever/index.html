Study 10 · Sea of Grass

Fever

An examination of one street: pastel houses down both sides of a grey road, under a hard blue sky, going on forever. Press Fever and cast a spell on it. Cyclone is a black eye that rides over the surfaces of things and twists, sinks and tears them as it goes; Shatter breaks whatever it passes into the pieces it's made of (bricks, boards, shingles, turf, asphalt), which burst off, tumble and fall, then fly back; Liquify ripples it, melts it into drips and sets worms crawling over it, all at once, in whatever mix you like. Choose with the spell button beside Fever.

fever
the button, or F: casts the spell; press again and the street comes back whole
steer
the spell sits on whatever is under the pointer and rides over it: the walls, roofs and lawn around it are wrung or broken; click and it surges
pick
in controls, press Pick (or K), then click one thing: a colour of house, the lamps, the road, the lawn. The spell then hits only that, and clicking casts it. Pick again to add more; Clear lets it hit everything
spell
the button beside Fever opens the list of spells; on the sky, each has a flat form
controls
turn the spell's dials, the speed you walk at, and under Cast: surge, freeze, follow

Fever is the picture-treating tool from Fantasmagoria, the studio's live show, tried out here on its own. The street comes from a popular dreamcore meme: an endless suburban street of pastel houses under a too-blue sky. It is drawn live with three.js. Cyclone is after a TouchDesigner piece posted to r/TouchDesigner ("using TD to eat up worlds"), rebuilt in WebGL; Shatter after the Voronoi-fracture pieces common in TouchDesigner, and Liquify after its fluid displacement work and Fabio Catapano's Spectrum 001. Nothing leaves your browser.
