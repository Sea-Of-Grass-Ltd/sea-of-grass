Study 10 · Sea of Grass

Fever

An examination of one street: pastel houses down both sides of a grey road, under a hard blue sky, going on forever. Press Fever and cast a spell on it. The first spell is Dark Puddle, a portal that drags the street, row by row, into a black eye you steer.

fever
the button, or F: casts the spell; press again and the street comes back whole
steer
move the pointer and the eye follows; click and it swallows
pick
in controls, turn on Pick (or K) and click things: a colour of house, the lamps, the road, the lawn, the sky. The spell then hits only those
controls
pick the spell, turn its dials, or swap the street for a film of it

Fever is the picture-treating tool from Fantasmagoria, the studio's live show, tried out here on its own. The street is drawn live with three.js; the film of it was made with Higgsfield from a still. The portal is after a TouchDesigner piece posted to r/TouchDesigner ("using TD to eat up worlds"), rebuilt in WebGL. Nothing leaves your browser.
