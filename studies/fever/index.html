Study 10 · Sea of Grass

Fever

An examination of one street: pastel houses down both sides of a grey road, under a hard blue sky, going on forever. Press Fever and cast a spell on it. Cyclone is a black eye that rides over the surfaces of things and twists, sinks and tears them as it goes; Shatter breaks whatever it passes into shards that burst off, tumble and fall, then fly back. Choose with the spell button beside Fever.

fever
the button, or F: casts the spell; press again and the street comes back whole
steer
the spell sits on whatever is under the pointer and rides over it: the walls, roofs and lawn around it are wrung or broken; click and it surges
pick
in controls, press Pick (or K), then click one thing: a colour of house, the lamps, the road, the lawn. The spell then hits only that, and clicking casts it. Pick again to add more; Clear lets it hit everything
spell
the button beside Fever opens the list of spells; on the sky, each has a flat form
controls
turn the spell's dials, or swap the street for a film of it

Fever is the picture-treating tool from Fantasmagoria, the studio's live show, tried out here on its own. The street comes from a popular dreamcore meme: an endless suburban street of pastel houses under a too-blue sky. It is drawn live with three.js; the film of it was made with Higgsfield from a still. Cyclone is after a TouchDesigner piece posted to r/TouchDesigner ("using TD to eat up worlds"), rebuilt in WebGL; Shatter after the Voronoi-fracture pieces common in TouchDesigner. Nothing leaves your browser.
