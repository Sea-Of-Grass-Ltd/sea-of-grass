# Websites page as a Short, six cards (written before the build)

Tristan approved the combined board on 2026-11-19 (Fable's critique plus our draft). The angle: "Your website runs itself from your register." It sells less work, not more business.
Tests in `websites.test.mjs` walk these steps at 360, 390 and 1440 wide.

## Cards (one line each, art on every one)

1. Your website can run itself from your register. (kicker: Your website, fixed and looked after. Art: the full-screen live meadow. The shop preloads.)
2. Go on. Tap Sold out. (Art: the live register and the real Bunny Burritos page. After the tap, the caption becomes "That's the whole job. You never log in to anything.")
3. That's one less call asking what's left. (Art: the meadow growing in, and the counter phone image once it exists.)
4. Nine shops we built to show you. (Art: all nine live shop pages in a sideways rail with a "1 of 9" count.)
5. $50 a month, and Tristan and Bryce look after it. (Fix $250, sites from $1,200, custom from $2,000. Tapping $1,200 shows the $23-a-week line, word for word.)
6. Be one of our first five shops. (Art: a Bunny World demo photo, captioned as one. Five flowers, with as many open as there are spots left. One button: Book 15 minutes with Tristan or Bryce. Then the phone number and the safety line. The header's Book button hides here. "Read the details" opens the rest.)

## Steps

1. Land on card 1, with the bar's first of six segments filled.
2. Move one card at a time by swipe, tap (right side forward, left side back), wheel, and the arrow keys, Space and Page Up/Down.
3. Tap Sold out on card 2: the live shop flips and the caption swaps, and the card doesn't move.
4. Swipe the nine shops on card 4: the count follows, and the card doesn't move.
5. Tap $1,200 on card 5: the $23 line appears, and the card doesn't move.
6. On card 6, check the honest spots count and flowers, the one button, the phone number, the safety line and the honest photo caption. "Read the details" gives every price (biggest first, with buy by card), the email, the founding offer, the hookup and every question.

## Rules at every width
- 6 cards, one line each of 15 words or fewer, and each line fits on screen. Art on every card covers at least a quarter of the screen.
- Cards that aren't on screen can't be tabbed into. No sideways scroll, no console errors, no CSS gradients, no oversell, no free month.
