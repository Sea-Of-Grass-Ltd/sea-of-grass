# Websites page, two paths (written before the build)

Fable's plan, approved by Tristan on 2026-11-19 ("Approved, build Fable's page"). It's a normal, naturally scrolling page. Decided buyers buy or book straight away and can read the fine print; unsure visitors get an optional pitch below.
Tests in `websites.test.mjs` walk these steps at 360, 390 and 1440 wide.

## Sections
1. **Hero** (Dew, Manrope): "Your website can run itself from your register." One price line, "Fixes $250 · care $50 a month · sites from $1,200". Buttons "See prices and buy" (to Plans) and "Book 15 minutes", a text link "Not sure yet? Watch it work ↓", and a live meadow along the foot with no hint text.
2. **Plans** (Dew): "Every price, out in the open." Rows for Fix $250, Care $50 a month (with the founding-five strip: the real count, $50 from day one, locked for two years, a free photo shoot, owe nothing at the hookup, an honest review) and Sites from $1,200 (with the $23-a-week line). Custom from $2,000 sits beneath, plus a jump link to "Hookup · Questions". Buy buttons live only here.
3. **Demo** (Ember's Syne): "Go on. Tap Sold out." The live register beside the real Bunny Burritos page.
4. **Calls** (Dusk's Instrument Serif): "That's one less call asking what's left." The counter-phone picture, three questions and answers as text, then the more-business answer.
5. **Bunny World** (Clay's Bricolage): "Nine shops we built to show you." All nine live shops in a sideways rail, each labelled in its own face.
6. **Hooking up, plainly** (Ledger's Libre Caslon): three steps, what we see and never see, and the owe-nothing line.
7. **Before you buy** (Dew): every question, including the catch and Facebook.
8. **Close** (Dew): "Talk to Tristan or Bryce." The meadow, one Book button and the phone number as text.

## Steps
1. Land: the line, every price and both paths are on screen, with nothing to tap.
2. Decided buyer: one tap on "See prices and buy" puts a Buy button on screen.
3. The page scrolls like a page (wheel, Page Down); nothing is hijacked.
4. Unsure visitor: tap Sold out, read the calls, swipe the shops, read the hookup and the answers.
5. At every scroll position there's never more than one Book button in view. The header Book appears once the hero's has gone.
