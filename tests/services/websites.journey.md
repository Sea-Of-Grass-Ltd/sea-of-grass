# Websites page in the rhythm of Dribbble Hiring (written before the build)

Tristan, 2026-11-19: "everything flows with just key words and it does the salespitch (like the reels idea) and so on, with just a normal scrollable page". The model is Dribbble Hiring, Refero `0797edc6`: we take its rhythm, not its look. Also: "these massive walls of text are unwieldy", so the page is held to about half its words (1,152 before, 600 at most).

## Rhythm
1. **Top** (Dew, Manrope): the headline, one line, then two plan cards side by side. Care $50 a month is recommended (thin accent border, badge, ticks, the founding five in two lines with the real count). Sites from $1,200 has ticks and the $23 line. Under them, a small line: Fix $250 (buyable) and custom from $2,000. Then "Not sure yet? Watch it work ↓", a Book link, the fine-print jump, and the meadow at the foot.
2. **Proof row:** the nine Bunny World shop names, each in its own type, labelled as our demo shops. They're never presented as clients.
3. **Pitch:** alternating two-column sections with a few key words each. The demo (Syne) has the phone on the left; the calls (Instrument Serif) have the picture on the right. Bunny World (Bricolage) shows nine live shops, and the hookup (Libre Caslon) has three one-line points plus the owe-nothing line, with "what we can and never see" behind a toggle.
4. **Benefits:** Two-minute hookup, Owe nothing if you back out, Fixed price in writing, Cancel any time, each with an icon.
5. **End:** 6–8 questions with one- or two-sentence answers, then "Talk to Tristan or Bryce." with one Book button and the phone number.

## Steps
1. Land: the headline, both plans and a Buy button are on screen, with no taps.
2. The page scrolls like a page.
3. Unsure visitors read down the pitch, tap Sold out, swipe the shops and open the toggles.
4. There's never more than one Book button in view.
