# Websites page: the owner's journey (written before the rebuild)

Tests in `websites.test.mjs` walk these steps at 360, 390 and 1440 wide.
Tristan approved the structure on 2026-11-19 ("Approved, yes to owe-nothing, write the tests").

## Steps

1. **Land.** The headline says we run the site from the register they already use. The facts row in the hero shows public prices: $250 fixes, $50 a month care and sites from $1,200.
2. **Recognise the problem.** "Sound familiar?" lists owner quotes, including the register one: they changed a price at the counter and the website still shows the old one.
3. **Try the register.** The register demo sits beside the real Bunny Burritos site, running live. Tapping "Sold out" on the horchata turns the site's horchata button to "Sold out" and disables it. Raising the Big Carrot burrito to $13 changes the site's button to $13.00. Tapping "Sold out" again puts it back.
4. **See which registers work.** Square is ready now. Shopify and Clover are named honestly as set up per shop, never as ready-made.
5. **See the proof.** Bunny World: nine working shops on one street, with the live showcase and a link to walk in.
6. **Lose the nerves about hooking up.** "Hooking up, plainly" says what it involves, what changes for them and how to undo it (one click in the Square Dashboard). It lists what we can and can't see, and promises that if they change their mind at the hookup they owe nothing.
7. **See every price, biggest first.** The buy block comes after the hookup section. Its first price is the whole site at $2,000, then the starter site at $1,200, then the cards ($250 fix, $50 a month care) that can be bought by card.
8. **Know the steps and get answers.** The four steps are listed, the questions include the register ones, and the close offers a call with Tristan or Bryce.

## Page rules checked at every width

- Sections run in the approved order: hero, sound familiar, register, Bunny World, hookup, buy, how it goes, questions, close.
- The register's buttons are at least 44 px tall on phones.
- The register demo changes the real demo site; nothing is a picture.
- The page doesn't scroll sideways and logs no console errors.
- No CSS gradients are used as surfaces in the page's stylesheet.
- No section repeats the same button text twice.
