# Cat swing: layout sketch

A re-staging of Fragonard's *The Swing* (1767) inside a cat: one tabby curls round the frame,
its tail leaving the left edge and curling back over the top, its raised foreleg leaving the top
right and coming back down to hold a single string, wound round a great grey shrike that swings
over the open claws of its hind foot. The light lives in the space the cat encloses.
